EXPERIMENTAL SUMMARY
“Sometimes we respond to the work before we know who made it.”

This experiment explores how authorship, visual quality, micro-details, texture grain, and perceived effort shape audience response to modern creative output.

About The Experiment

From Human-Made to AI-Generated: Exploring How Creative Content Influences Audience Perception.

As synthetic generation tools rapidly advance, distinguishing between human craftsmanship and algorithmic synthesis challenges our intuitive assumptions about art, authenticity, and visual effort.

Across 15 rounds of increasing complexity, this test evaluates whether subtle micro-details, light diffusion, stroke chaos, and texture grain impact your ability to discern genuine human authorial intent.
